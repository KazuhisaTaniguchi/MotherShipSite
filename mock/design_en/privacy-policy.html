This English translation of the Privacy Policy is provided for reference only. If there is any discrepancy between this translation and the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

Kazuhisa Taniguchi (the “Operator”) handles information relating to the remote desktop app MOTHERSHIP and the website mothershipremote.com as set out in this Privacy Policy (the “Policy”), in accordance with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other applicable laws.

In a nutshell

  • Your screen, input, clipboard, files, audio, and voice are encrypted and exchanged directly between your Mac and your device. They do not pass through MOTHERSHIP servers. The Operator does not receive their contents.
  • There are no accounts. You never need to enter your name or email address in the apps.
  • The apps contain no analytics or advertising mechanisms.
  • Payments are handled by the App Store and Google Play. The Operator does not receive your credit card or similar information.
  • The only thing the Operator receives is what you send when you contact us. We use it to reply to you.

This summary is here to make the Policy easier to read. The official terms are set out in the sections below.

1.Our basic approach

MOTHERSHIP is an app for using your own Mac from somewhere else. A Mac's screen shows important information: work documents, email, photos, and more. That's why MOTHERSHIP is built to work without the Operator collecting users' information. As far as possible, the information it needs is handled only on your Mac and your device.

2.What this policy covers

This Policy applies to the following:

  1. MOTHERSHIP HOST, which is installed on the Mac being controlled
  2. MOTHERSHIP, which is installed on the iPhone, iPad, Android device, or Mac used to control it
  3. The website mothershipremote.com and its contact form

Below, 1 and 2 are together called the “Apps.” How other companies' services used together with the Apps handle information is governed by each company's own policies (see Section 9).

3.Information the apps handle

Information exchanged between your Mac and your device

Screen video, keyboard, mouse, and touch input, clipboard contents (text and images), files, your Mac's audio, and your voice during voice input are exchanged directly between your Mac and your device, and only while you're connected.

  • Communication is encrypted (TLS 1.3 and more). At home, you connect over your home network; away from home, you connect over Tailscale's encrypted network.
  • No Operator servers or relay servers are used. The Operator does not receive any of this content.
  • The Apps do not record the text you enter, your clipboard contents, or your passwords in their logs.
  • Your voice is not stored. It is sent from your device to your Mac, where it is handed to a dictation app (Aqua Voice).
  • Files you receive are saved to a folder such as Downloads on the receiving device.

Information stored on your devices

The Apps store the following information, which they need in order to work, only on your Mac and your device (the one exception is the list of Macs added to your License, which on iPhone and iPad is also stored in your iCloud). It is not sent to the Operator.

  • The names and connection addresses of registered Macs, and certificate information used to verify the connection
  • A key for each device. It is stored in the device's secure hardware (such as the Secure Enclave or Android Keystore) and cannot be extracted
  • The list of devices registered with MOTHERSHIP HOST (each device's name and the public key used to verify it)
  • Settings for picture quality, display, keyboard, and so on
  • The time the trial started and a flag indicating whether a License has been purchased (MOTHERSHIP HOST). So that the trial can't be restarted, this may remain in the Mac's keychain or elsewhere even after the app is deleted
  • The list of Macs added to your License (each Mac's ID, name, and the date it was added) and the date and time a Mac was removed without connecting (on the device). On iPhone and iPad, this is also stored in your iCloud so it can be shared among devices signed in to the same Apple ID

Information sent to the Operator

The Apps contain no mechanisms for account registration, usage analytics, advertising, or crash reporting. The Apps do not send information to the Operator.

5.Purchase information

  1. License purchases and payments are handled by the App Store and Google Play. The Operator does not receive your name, address, or payment information such as credit card details.
  2. After you make a purchase, the proof of purchase signed by the Store is sent from your device to the Host Mac and verified on the Mac itself. Neither the Operator's servers nor the internet is used for this verification.
  3. MOTHERSHIP HOST stores only a flag indicating that a License has been purchased and a value derived from the transaction number (a hash). It does not store information that can identify an individual.
  4. To count the Macs that one purchase can cover (up to three), your device stores, for each purchase, the list of Macs added to your License (see Section 3). The counting happens on your device; the Operator's servers are not used.
  5. How the Stores handle purchase information is governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

6.Website and inquiries

Information you give us when you contact us

Through the contact form, we receive the following information:

  • Your name and email address (required)
  • The type of inquiry, subject, and message (required)
  • Your device, your Mac's macOS version, and the app version (optional)
  • Attachments (optional; JPEG, PNG, or PDF; up to 5 MB each; up to three files)

What you send reaches the Operator as an email. We also send a confirmation email to the address you entered to let you know we received it. Please do not include passwords or other information used to log in or register.

About attachments

You can attach screenshots and the like. Attachments reach the Operator along with the inquiry email. They are kept on the website's server only temporarily while the email is being sent, and are deleted afterward.

Please do not send images that show passwords, QR codes, or other people's names or email addresses. If such information is visible, cover it up before attaching. Attachments are used only to reply to your inquiry and to investigate problems.

Access logs

Like any website, the server that hosts this site (Xserver) keeps access logs of the IP address, browser type, date and time, and so on of each visit. The Operator uses these only to keep the website running safely and to investigate unauthorized access.

Cookies and analytics

The website does not currently use analytics tools or advertising cookies. If we start using them, we will revise this Policy and let you know.

7.How we use information

The Operator uses the information it receives only for the following purposes:

  1. Replying to inquiries, and contacting you for that purpose
  2. Investigating the causes of problems and improving the Apps
  3. Sending important notices about the Apps or the website (such as changes to the Terms of Service or this Policy)
  4. Responding to unauthorized access and to use that violates the Terms of Service

We never use the email address you provide in an inquiry for advertising or newsletters.

8.Sharing with others

The Operator does not provide personal information to others without your consent, except in the following cases:

  1. When required by law
  2. When necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property and it is difficult to obtain the individual's consent
  3. When it is necessary to cooperate with a national or local government body in carrying out duties prescribed by law

Note that the Operator entrusts information to the server used for the website and email (Xserver).

10.Security measures

The Operator takes measures such as the following to prevent information from being leaked, lost, or altered:

  • Encrypting communication between Mac and device, and verifying the destination with certificates
  • Allowing only devices registered by QR code to connect. Repeated failed connection attempts are refused for a while
  • A feature that hides the Host Mac's screen while you're connected from outside, and locking on disconnect
  • Managing the password and settings of the account used for inquiry email

11.Retention and deletion

  1. Information stored on your devices (Section 3) is erased when you delete the Apps or unregister. However, trial information may remain on the Mac even after the app is deleted. In addition, the list of Macs added to your License may remain in iCloud after the app is deleted (so the count carries over when you reinstall the app with the same Apple ID).
  2. If you lose a device, delete it under Registered Devices in the MOTHERSHIP HOST menu.
  3. Inquiries and their attachments are kept only as long as needed to reply and for any follow-up, and are deleted once they are no longer needed.

12.Requests for disclosure, correction, and deletion

If you would like the Operator to disclose, correct, delete, or stop using information about you that it holds (such as the content of an inquiry), please contact us as described in Section 15. After confirming your identity, we will respond promptly in accordance with the law.

The Operator does not hold the information that the Apps store on your devices. You can erase it yourself through the Apps' settings or by deleting the Apps.

13.Children

The Apps are not directed at children. If you are a minor, please get the consent of a parent or guardian before sending an inquiry.

14.Changes to this policy

The Operator may change this Policy in response to changes in the law or in the Apps' features. For important changes, we will give advance notice on the website or elsewhere. The revised Policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.
