If you can't connect

First, check the following:

  • Is your Mac awake? (Turn on “Keep Mac awake while waiting.”)
  • Does MOTHERSHIP HOST have Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions on your Mac?
  • At home: are your Mac and your device on the same Wi-Fi?
  • Away from home: is Tailscale signed in to the same account on both?

Purchases and refunds

Payments and refunds are handled by the App Store and Google Play. To ask for a refund, contact the store where you made your purchase. If you switch devices, use Restore Purchase in the app to bring your license along.

    For bug reports

    Including the following helps us find the cause sooner:

    • The model of the Mac you control, and its macOS version
    • The device you control it from, and its OS version
    • Whether you're on home Wi-Fi or away from home (Tailscale)
    • What you were doing, and what happened
    • Any message shown in the MOTHERSHIP HOST menu

    About our reply

    We review each message and reply in the order received. It may take a few days to get back to you, and replies may be slower on weekends and Japanese public holidays.

    Don't write or show passwords

    Please don't include information used to log in or register, such as your Mac's password, your Tailscale password, or an image of a QR code. Make sure none of it appears in attached images, either. We will never ask you for it.
