Features

More than mirroring.
Made for real work.

MOTHERSHIP is a remote desktop app built for using your Mac from afar, not just looking at it. Reading text on a small screen, controlling things by touch, writing by voice: we sweated the details on every one.

1 See it clearly

Small screen.
Sharp text.

Mirror a 27-inch monitor onto a phone, and the text is far too small to read. While you're connected, MOTHERSHIP adds a dedicated display to your Mac that matches the exact size and shape of your device. It fills your screen pixel for pixel, so tiny terminal text and the numbers in a table stay crisp.

  • Hold your device upright for a tall display, or sideways for a wide one. Either way, it fits edge to edge. In portrait, expand a window to read it at full width.
  • The moment the screen stops moving, it's re-sent in high quality. Text stays clean while you read what your AI wrote.
  • Lots of fine print? Switch the picture quality to Text Priority.
  • Pinch to zoom, and the area you're looking at is recaptured at a higher resolution.
  • You can also mirror the monitor you normally use, just as it is.

The dedicated display disappears automatically when you disconnect. Rotating your device doesn't rearrange your Mac's windows (that's standard Mac behavior). When windows overlap, swipe up with three fingers to see them all and pick one.

2 Control it by touch

No mouse? Run your Mac with your fingers.

With Direct Touch, wherever you tap is where you click. It feels like touching your Mac's screen itself. When your AI asks for confirmation, just tap the button.

There's a keyboard, too (see “A proper Mac keyboard, too.” below).

  • Tap to click, touch and hold to right-click, scroll with two fingers.
  • Swipe up with three fingers for Mission Control (all your open windows). Swipe down for the current app's windows. Swipe left or right to switch desktops (on Android, in trackpad mode).
  • Pick, expand, and close windows. You can tap the buttons in a window's top-left corner directly.
  • For precise targets, switch to a mode that turns the whole screen into a trackpad.

MOTHERSHIP works with Aqua Voice, a dictation app for Mac. Speak to the device in your hand, your voice reaches your Mac, and the words appear in whatever app is open. Leave it running

Your Mac stays awake. If the connection drops, it's right back.

When your Mac goes to sleep, the work you handed off stops, and so do connections from outside. MOTHERSHIP HOST has a setting that keeps your Mac awake while it waits. From your device, you connect the same way whether you're at home or out.

To connect from outside your home, install the Tailscale app on both your Mac and your device and sign in to the same account on each. Mac laptops may go to sleep when the lid is closed.

  • In MOTHERSHIP HOST, turn on “Keep Mac awake while waiting” and “Open at Login.”
  • On your home Wi-Fi, MOTHERSHIP finds your registered Mac and connects automatically.
  • Away from home, you connect through Tailscale, a VPN service that's free for personal use. No router setup required.
  • If you switch from Wi-Fi to cellular, it reconnects on its own.
  • While the screen is still, almost no data is sent. Data usage adapts automatically to your connection.
  • Three picture-quality modes: Balanced (up to 60 fps), Text Priority, and Smoothness Priority.
  • After a period of inactivity, the connection closes automatically (30 minutes by default).

Ordinary screen sharing shrinks your Mac's monitor to fit. MOTHERSHIP adds one more display to your Mac, shaped just like the screen in your hand. Text looks the size it does when you're sitting at your Mac.

Keep private screens private.

While you're connected from somewhere else, the screen of your Mac at home is in plain view of anyone nearby. MOTHERSHIP can black out the Mac's own screen and show it only on your device.

  • When you connect from outside your home, the screen is hidden automatically. If it was hidden, your Mac locks when you disconnect.
  • Everything is encrypted (TLS 1.3 and more). Video and input travel directly between your Mac and your device, never through MOTHERSHIP servers.
  • Pair with a QR code. Only devices you've registered can connect, and each device's key can't be extracted from the device's secure hardware.
  • Lock the app behind Face ID, Touch ID, or your fingerprint.
  • Your Mac notifies you whenever a device connects or disconnects.
  • No router setup (port forwarding) needed.

Frequently asked questions

Foldable phones

Which foldable phones does it work on?

It's built to run on foldable phones with Android 13 or later. So far, we've tested it on an actual Galaxy Z Fold7. We'll announce other models as we test them.

Can I use it on the cover screen?

Yes. With the phone folded, the cover screen shows your Mac's screen and a small trackpad so you can check on progress. In your Galaxy's settings, allow apps to keep running on the cover screen when you close the phone.

How do I use it half-folded?

While you're connected, fold the phone halfway and set it on a desk. The top half becomes your Mac's screen, and the bottom half becomes shortcuts and a trackpad (tap ⌨ to switch to the keyboard). Hold it like a book, and your Mac's screen is on the left with the trackpad and keyboard on the right.

How is this different from Samsung DeX or ordinary screen sharing?

DeX lets you use your phone's apps on a big, desktop-style screen. MOTHERSHIP goes the other way: it lets you control your Mac at home from your phone. And where ordinary screen sharing shrinks your Mac's screen, MOTHERSHIP adds a display to your Mac in the same shape as the screen in your hand, so text appears at a size you can actually read.

Can I control Claude Code or Codex while I'm out?

Yes. MOTHERSHIP shows your Mac's screen as it is and lets you control it, so terminal-based tools like Claude Code and Codex work just as they always do. To connect from outside your home, install Tailscale on your Mac and your phone. MOTHERSHIP has no AI features of its own.

AI and everyday use

Which AI tools does it work with?

Any AI that runs on your Mac. MOTHERSHIP shows your Mac's screen as it is and lets you control it. Terminal-based AI, the AI in your editor, the AI in your browser: they all work exactly as they do at your desk. There's nothing to configure on the AI side. MOTHERSHIP has no AI features of its own.

Will it notify me when my AI finishes?

No. MOTHERSHIP shows your Mac's screen and lets you control it; it doesn't have a feature that tells you when a job is done. The idea is to open it whenever you're curious and see how things are going.

Can I really read terminal text on a phone-size screen?

Yes. Your Mac gets a dedicated display in the same shape as your device, so text isn't shrunk. Pinch to zoom when you want a closer look. For lots of fine print, the Text Priority picture-quality mode is a good fit.

How is it different from other remote desktop apps?

It's the way it shows your screen. Rather than mirroring your Mac's monitor as it is, MOTHERSHIP gives your Mac a dedicated display in the same shape as your device, so text isn't shrunk even on a small screen. You can also write by voice (it works with Aqua Voice), and it's a one-time purchase.

What kind of work is it good for?

Work where you need a good look at text and the screen: checking on jobs you've handed to AI and giving the next instruction, programming, working in documents or a browser, reviewing designs. It's not suited to fast-action games.

Does it work on Android tablets?

Yes. The same app installs on tablets running Android 13 or later, from the same Google Play listing. On a tablet it works like the iPad app, with a virtual display shaped to the screen and a larger keyboard.

Can it control Windows PCs?

No. It controls Macs only. You can control your Mac from an iPhone, iPad, Android phone or tablet, or another Mac.

Voice input

What do I need for voice input?

On your Mac, install Aqua Voice (a dictation app from another company) and BlackHole 2ch (a free virtual audio driver), then set Aqua Voice's microphone to BlackHole 2ch. After that, just talk while holding the 🎤 button on your device.

Can I use MOTHERSHIP without Aqua Voice?

Yes. Aqua Voice is needed only for voice input. Everything else, such as viewing the screen, controlling by touch, and typing on the keyboard, works without it.

Is my voice stored anywhere?

MOTHERSHIP doesn't store your voice. Audio is sent directly from your device to your Mac, where it's handed to Aqua Voice. For how Aqua Voice handles audio, please see Aqua Voice's own documentation.

Away from home and Mac status

Can I use it when I'm out?

Yes. Install Tailscale (a VPN service that's free for personal use) on your Mac and your device, and you can connect from anywhere. You don't need to change any router settings.

Do I have to leave my Mac on while I'm out?

Yes. If your Mac goes to sleep, you can't connect from outside. Turn on “Keep Mac awake while waiting” in MOTHERSHIP HOST. Mac laptops may go to sleep when the lid is closed.

What if my Mac is locked?

With MOTHERSHIP for Mac, you can unlock the Mac you're connecting to by entering its password.

Does it work after my Mac restarts?

Until you log in to your Mac, MOTHERSHIP HOST isn't running, so you can't connect. Once you've logged in, it starts listening automatically (if Open at Login is turned on).

How much data does it use?

It adjusts automatically to what's happening on screen and to your connection. While the screen is still, almost no data is sent.

Can several devices connect at the same time?

Only one device can be connected to a Mac at a time. If you connect from another device, the connection switches to that one.

Security

Do my screen and input pass through someone's server?

Not through MOTHERSHIP servers. At home, your Mac and your device talk directly over your home network. Away from home, they talk directly over Tailscale's encrypted network.

Do I need to create an account?

MOTHERSHIP has no accounts. Scan the QR code shown on your Mac and you're ready to go. You'll need a Tailscale account only if you want to connect from outside your home.

What permissions does my Mac need?

Two: Screen Recording (to show your Mac's screen) and Accessibility (to pass along keyboard and mouse input). You can open the right settings pane from the MOTHERSHIP HOST menu.

What if I lose my device?

In the MOTHERSHIP HOST menu, open Registered Devices and delete that device. Once it's deleted, it can no longer connect.

Pricing

Is there a monthly fee?

No. After the 14-day trial, a single purchase is all you need.

I have two Macs. Do I need to buy it twice?

No. One purchase covers up to three Macs.

What if I replace a Mac, or want to use it with a fourth one?

On your device, go to Settings > License and remove a Mac you no longer use. That frees up a slot for another Mac. You can remove a Mac without connecting to it only once every 90 days. Before you part with a Mac, it's safest to remove its license first, using “Remove license from this Mac” in the MOTHERSHIP HOST menu.
